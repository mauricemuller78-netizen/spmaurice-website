Football Philosophy

Principles

Without the ball

Relentless Pressure

Without the ball, the objective is to aggressively hunt possession. I favour intense man-to-man pressure, forcing opponents into uncomfortable situations and constantly searching for opportunities to regain the ball.

Pressing should not be passive. The team actively searches for triggers and opportunities to attack the opponent’s possession. The mentality is: Pressing. Pressing. Pressing.

With the ball

Freedom Within Structure

In possession, I prefer freedom over overly rigid positional occupation. Players should be able to roam, rotate and leave their nominal positions when they recognise an advantage.

When one player leaves a space, another player should recognise the movement and occupy, protect or compensate for the resulting space. Structure exists to support freedom rather than restrict it.
