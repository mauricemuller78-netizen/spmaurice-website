Liverpool 2018/19: Champions of Europe

Jürgen Klopp's 2018/19 Champions League-winning Liverpool. A tactical analysis of the pressing, transitions, attacking structure and mentality behind their European success.

Season Overview

Liverpool's 2018/19 season was the year Jürgen Klopp's project reached the highest level in Europe. The team had already developed a clear identity in the previous seasons, but this campaign added more control and consistency to the intensity that had defined Liverpool under Klopp.

In the Premier League, Liverpool produced an extraordinary title challenge and finished with 97 points, losing only one league match all season. Even that was not enough to win the title, as Manchester City finished one point ahead. The fact that a 97-point season still ended in second place shows the level of the domestic battle Liverpool were involved in.

In Europe, the season became historic. Liverpool recovered from a 3–0 first-leg defeat against Barcelona in the Champions League semi-final by winning the return leg 4–0 at Anfield. They then beat Tottenham Hotspur 2–0 in the final to win the club's sixth European Cup.

What made the season so important was not only the trophy itself, but the development of the team. Liverpool were still capable of playing with extreme intensity, pressing aggressively and attacking transitions at speed, but they had also become more mature in possession and more comfortable controlling different types of matches.
