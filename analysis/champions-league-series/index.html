Champions League Series

Twenty years of European champions, analysed through the ideas that made them different. The Champions League Series breaks down each winner’s in-possession structures, pressing, defensive organisation, key player roles and the decisive tactical details behind their success.

The goal is not simply to revisit famous teams, but to understand why they won — how their systems worked, how they adapted to opponents and which principles separated them from the rest of Europe.
